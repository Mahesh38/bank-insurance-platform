# 02 — Vision, goals & principles

**Sources:** Volume 01, Volume 02, Phase 1

---

## Vision statement

> Build a **bank-owned, insurer-agnostic insurance distribution platform** that owns the complete insurance distribution journey from **lead creation through policy issuance**. Initially integrate with **1SilverBullet** where needed and progressively transition to **direct insurer integrations** while preserving a stable business model.

---

## Primary goal (BG core)

Create a single, consistent, compliant and measurable customer journey from lead through policy issuance, while allowing the bank to independently evolve products, integrations and operating model.

### Goal clusters (narrative)

| Cluster | Intent |
|---------|--------|
| Customer experience | Simple digital journey; less re-entry; transparent comparison; assisted + self-service; consistent UX across insurers |
| RM | One workspace for customers, leads, suitability, recommendations, quotes, proposals |
| Growth | Penetration, conversion, shorter cycles, rapid product launch, simpler insurer onboarding |
| Ops excellence | Standardized workflows, automation, real-time status, auditability |
| Governance | Consent, rule governance, audit trails, configurable regulatory controls, secure PII |
| Tech independence | Capabilities independent of provider; 1SB now → direct later without behaviour change |
| Analytics | RM / branch / ops / exec dashboards; conversion, SLA, insurer performance |
| Future readiness | Multi-LOB, configurable product rules, new channels |

---

## Guiding principles

1. Business before technology  
2. Canonical business model  
3. Configuration over code  
4. Replaceable integrations  
5. Security by design  
6. Traceability  
7. Observability  

Phase 1 restates ownership: platform owns **customer journey, lead, consent, suitability and reporting**; providers are implementation details.

## Scope framing

**In (capability intent):** Customer, RM, Lead, Consent, Suitability, Product Catalogue, Quote, Proposal, Underwriting tracking, Payment, Policy, Reporting, Administration, Integration Hub, Identity/Audit.

**Explicit exclusions (Vol 01):** Insurer core systems; claims administration.

**Tension to resolve:** Phase 1 lists Renewals & Servicing as a capability; Vol 01 excludes claims. Treat **renewals/servicing as later-phase platform capability**, claims as out unless reopened.
