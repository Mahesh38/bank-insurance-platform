## 2. Purpose

The Create Pitch Deck Module enables a Bank SP or Insurance RM to generate a customer-specific Savings product presentation, preview it and share it with an existing bank customer. The module supports two entry scenarios: an existing-lead flow where customer and product context are already available, and a product-first flow where the seller selects the customer before generating the deck. In the product-first flow, successful initiation of sharing creates a Diary Lead. No SP or Insurance RM assignment is captured within this module; assignment is completed later when formal customer onboarding begins.

## 3. Business Objectives

- Provide the seller with an approved customer-specific product conversation aid.
- Use existing customer, seller and product context wherever already available.
- Allow product-first customer engagement without forcing early Suitability or formal onboarding.
- Create a Diary Lead only when a product-first pitch deck is actually sent to the customer.
- Preserve the same Lead ID when the customer later enters the formal Savings buying journey.
- Support secure preview, download and multichannel sharing.
- Maintain clear separation between Pitch Deck Product, Suitable Products, selected Buy Now product and final BI product.

## 10. Customer Onboarding After Product-First Sharing

- When the customer later enters the formal buying journey, the seller shall open the existing Diary Lead.
- The normal customer onboarding journey shall capture the required SP or Insurance RM counterpart assignment.
- The same Lead ID shall continue; onboarding shall not create a fresh lead.
- Savings Suitability shall be completed before the normal product-selection and BI journey.
- The Pitch Deck Product may or may not be returned by Suitability.
- The customer may later select another suitable or otherwise eligible product according to the Listing rules.
- The platform shall separately retain Pitch Deck Product, Suitable Products, Buy Now Product and Final BI Product.
- The pitch deck remains in Lead history even if another product is finally selected.

## 17. Secure Document Sharing

- The link shall open only the intended Pitch Deck version.
- The URL shall not expose Lead ID, Customer ID, mobile, email or other sensitive data.
- The link shall not provide access to the seller platform session.
- Validity, authentication and expiry shall follow bank Information Security requirements.
- Approved communication templates shall be used and shall not be freely edited by the seller.
- Channel-level delivery references and results shall be retained.
- The Proposer registered contacts shall be used; Life Assured contacts, if any, are not applicable.
## 18. Diary Lead Creation Transaction

### 18.1 Trigger

BR-DIARY-001: In the product-first flow, the Diary Lead shall be created when at least one selected sharing channel accepts the sharing request.

### 18.2 Transaction Behaviour

- Customer, product, Pitch Deck version, sharing request and seller context shall be validated before initiation.
- The platform shall generate one Lead ID and link it to the accepted sharing event.
- The Lead and sharing event shall be committed consistently.
- If Lead creation fails after a channel accepts the request, the event shall be placed into a controlled reconciliation process using the same idempotency reference.
- Retry shall recover or link the same business event and shall not generate another Lead.
- The success message shall show the created Lead ID.
### 18.3 Lead Reporting

- The Pitch Deck lead is a Diary Lead and shall not count as an Eligible Lead.
- It may be included in operational counts of all Lead records and Pitch Deck engagement.
- It becomes Eligible only after a successful customer-specific BI later in the normal journey.

## 28. Key Business Rules Summary

KBR-01: Two flows exist: Existing Lead and Product First.

KBR-02: Existing Lead uses the existing Lead ID and customer.

KBR-03: Product First requires Customer 360 search and confirmation.

KBR-04: Only existing bank customers may receive a customer-specific deck.

KBR-05: No Lead is created on search, generation, preview or download.

KBR-06: Product-first sharing creates a Diary Lead only when at least one channel accepts the request.

KBR-07: The Pitch Deck module does not ask for SP/RM assignment.

KBR-08: Diary Lead is initially associated with the logged-in seller.

KBR-09: Assignment is captured later when formal customer onboarding starts.

KBR-10: The same Lead ID continues into onboarding and the buying journey.

KBR-11: The Pitch Deck Product is not automatically the final selected product.

KBR-12: Savings Suitability remains mandatory before final quote and BI.

KBR-13: Email, SMS and WhatsApp sharing are supported.

KBR-14: Previewed and shared deck must be the same immutable version.

KBR-15: Secure customer-specific document controls are mandatory.

KBR-16: The product-first Lead remains New, BI No, Suitability Not Started and non-Eligible.

KBR-17: Request Callback uses the same Lead and does not create another.

KBR-18: Pitch-deck resend does not create a duplicate Lead.

KBR-19: Pitch Deck generation and sharing do not count as BI generation.

KBR-20: All material actions and outcomes are audited.
