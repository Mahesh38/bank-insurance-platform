## 2. Purpose

This BRD defines the journey from Proposal Form initiation through seller-assisted data capture, customer review and editing, mandatory document review, consent and Standing Instruction authorisation, insurer-managed PIVC, first-premium payment through IFT, Payment Gateway or Cheque, post-payment eMandate, proposal submission to the insurer, additional-document upload, feedback and referral. The Proposal Form and its validations are dynamically rendered from insurer or partner APIs.

## 3. Business Objectives

- Allow the seller to complete an insurer-specific Proposal Form without hardcoding one common form.
- Handoff the same Application ID securely to the customer for review, permitted edits and consent.
- Keep final product, rider, premium and BI information read-only and unchanged during customer verification.
- Capture both SI authorisation and eMandate for Annual Premium policies at their approved journey points.
- Support IFT, Payment Gateway and Cheque for first-premium payment.
- Submit the complete proposal and payment context to the insurer and track subsequent requirements.
- Allow post-submission document upload and optional customer feedback and referral.

## 6. End-to-End Flow

STEP-01: Seller selects Continue to Proposal Form after final BI and applicable assignment.

STEP-02: Application ID is generated and linked to Lead and BI.

STEP-03: Proposal sections render dynamically from API.

STEP-04: Seller completes mandatory sections and Agent Confidentiality Report.

STEP-05: Platform sends secure verification link to the customer mobile and email.

STEP-06: Customer authenticates and reviews the same Application ID.

STEP-07: Customer edits API-permitted proposal fields and completes mandatory data.

STEP-08: Customer opens/downloads mandatory PDFs and accepts declarations.

STEP-09: For Annual Premium, customer selects an eligible AU account and authorises SI.

STEP-10: Customer completes OTP consent.

STEP-11: Customer is redirected to insurer-managed PIVC and completion status returns.

STEP-12: Customer selects first-premium method: IFT, Payment Gateway or Cheque.

STEP-13: After applicable payment status, Annual Premium customer completes eMandate.

STEP-14: Platform submits proposal to insurer when configured status conditions are met.

STEP-15: Insurer may request additional documents; customer uploads them.

STEP-16: Customer may provide feedback and refer a friend/family member.

STEP-17: Insurer underwriting continues toward issuance, pending requirements or rejection.

## 7. Application ID and Proposal Status

- Application ID is generated only when Proposal Form initiation succeeds.
- It remains linked to the same Lead ID, final BI Reference and BI Version.
- Repeated initiation resumes the existing Application ID.
- Lead Stage changes to Proposal Form Pending when the Proposal Form starts.
- Proposal statuses shall include Not Started, In Progress, Seller Completed, Customer Verification In Progress, Customer Consent Completed, PIVC Pending/Completed, Payment Pending/Completed, eMandate Pending/Completed, Submitted to Insurer and Insurer Processing, subject to API mapping.

- Generate a secure customer-verification link only after seller completion succeeds.
- Send the link to the Proposer registered mobile number and email ID.

- The link shall not expose Lead, Customer or Application identifiers in the URL.
- Validity, authentication and resend rules shall follow approved Information Security controls.
- Customer handoff status and delivery result shall be visible to the seller.
- Failure to deliver through one channel shall not recreate the application.
## 10. Customer Review and Editing

### 10.1 Customer Permissions

- Customer may review all applicable sections and edit only fields identified as customer-editable by API.
- Customer ID, Lead ID, Application ID, insurer, product, plan option, premium, PPT, Policy Term, return preference, riders, rider terms, total premium and BI are read-only.
- Customer edits remain under the same Application ID and create a new Proposal Version where applicable.
- Customer can Save and Resume before final consent.
- Dependent proposal questions shall refresh through API when an editable answer changes.
- Customer cannot proceed to consent until all mandatory applicable fields are valid.
### 10.2 No Quote or BI Impact

- No customer proposal edit triggers quote recalculation, rider revalidation, premium change or BI regeneration.
- The existing BI Reference and Version remain valid.
- Product, rider and premium information cannot be changed by the customer.
- The latest Proposal Version, not a new BI, is used for consent, PIVC, payment and insurer submission.
### 10.3 Edit Lock

- Customer may edit until final consent and OTP verification are successfully completed.
- After consent, the Proposal Version is locked and view-only.
- PIVC uses the locked Proposal Version.
- Any later correction follows an insurer or operational process outside this BRD.

### 32.1.9 Mandatory Document Review Rules

- Consent remains a separate explicit action.
- If a mandatory document version changes before consent, the customer must view or download the new version before consent.

### 32.1.17 Additional Document Requirements and Versioning

- The platform shall support multiple simultaneous insurer requirements, each with its own Requirement ID, document type, mandatory count, due date, instructions and status.
- A requirement may allow one or more files according to API metadata.
- Partial completion shall be displayed requirement by requirement.
- If a submitted document is rejected, the customer may re-upload when the API permits.
- Every re-upload creates a new document version; earlier files remain in secured audit history and are not overwritten.
- Only the latest submitted/accepted version is current.
- Validate extension, MIME type, size, corruption, password protection, malware and duplicate file conditions.
- Use secure storage, restricted preview/download, approved retention and no public URLs.
### 32.1.18 Feedback Trigger and Submission Rules

- Feedback shall be shown only after the customer has completed required actions and the application has reached the configured successful submission/completion page.
- Do not show Feedback while the journey is blocked at consent, PIVC, payment, mandate or mandatory document upload.
- Feedback is optional and Skip/Close shall be available.
- Only one active submitted Feedback record shall be accepted per Application ID unless an explicit edit flow is introduced.
- Closing without submission has no business impact.
### 32.1.19 Referral Privacy and Duplicate Controls

- The Referral form creates a Referral Record and Referral ID, not a Lead.
- The customer shall accept a Privacy/Referral declaration confirming that the referred person has been informed that their contact details may be shared with the bank for follow-up. Final wording requires Legal and Privacy approval.
- The declaration checkbox shall not be preselected and shall be mandatory for referral submission.
- Duplicate checks should consider referred mobile number, referring Customer ID and a configurable time window, and may also identify an existing customer, referral or lead.
- Duplicate handling shall prevent repeated referral records or display the existing referral outcome as configured.
- Conversion of a Referral Record into a Lead and outreach consent are outside this BRD.

## 33. Key Business Rules Summary
