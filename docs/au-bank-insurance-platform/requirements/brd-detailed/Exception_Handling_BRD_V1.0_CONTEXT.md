## 2. Purpose

To define the business, functional, data, validation, approval, notification, status, integration, reporting, audit and testing requirements for configurable Life Insurance exception handling within AUBIMA.

## 3. Business Objectives

- Evaluate each active exception rule using customer-linked data available in DWH, data migration and approved source systems. Annual premium shall be evaluated using the value captured during the journey.
- Prevent ineligible or incorrectly configured leads from progressing.
- Route approval-enabled exceptions through the applicable configured hierarchy.
- Provide controlled multi-level approvals with mandatory remarks and complete auditability.
- Allow the customer journey to resume only after the required final approval.
- Provide dashboards, notifications, MIS and operational traceability for each exception request.
## 4. Scope

### 4.1 In Scope

- Life Insurance rule evaluation during lead onboarding and suitability.
- Direct block and configurable exception-approval outcomes.
- Employee and branch-code-based hierarchy resolution.
- Branch Banking, Banca and Persistency hierarchy use.
- Multi-level sequential approval, Exception Lead Page, notifications, audit and MIS.
- Rule configuration governance, blocked Customer ID/Employee ID uploads, save/resume and rejected-lead treatment.
### 4.2 Out of Scope

- Fallback approvers, escalation timelines, automatic approvals and approval bypasses that are not explicitly configured or approved. The system shall not derive an alternate approver, skip a configured approval level, auto-approve a request, or bypass a blocked outcome.
- Lead Onboarding functionality unrelated to exception handling. Exception requests may be raised during or after lead onboarding based on data received from CBS, DWH and other approved APIs/source systems.
- A separate Exception Approval tab shall be available within the Lead section for all raisers and approvers, with Pending Approval, Approved and Rejected sub-tabs. Raisers shall view the current status of their requests, and approvers shall view requests assigned to them. A separate consolidated team view beyond these role-based views is out of scope.
- New rules or external data sources not described in the approved requirement. Leads generated through the Campaign Module or Lead Upload Module shall undergo exception-rule evaluation when the user resumes/initiates the onboarding journey; saving or initial generation alone shall not trigger evaluation or blocking.
### 4.3 Module Boundary

The boundary starts when onboarding is initiated, or a saved/generated lead is resumed. Saving a lead shall not trigger rule evaluation, approval routing or blocking. The boundary includes rule evaluation when onboarding is initiated/resumed and when annual premium becomes available, hierarchy resolution, approval processing and final treatment, and ends when the journey is released, remains blocked, is rejected, or the configured post-rejection treatment completes.

## 6. Exception Handling Overview and Entry Conditions

### 6.1 Exception Handling Objective

Detect applicable rule breaches and apply direct progression, block, or configured approval treatment.

### 6.2 Journey Trigger

Saving a lead stores it without executing exception rules. Start Onboarding executes rules whose required inputs are available. The High Premium rule is evaluated only after annual premium is captured at the relevant suitability step.

### 6.3 Entry Point

On initiation or resumption of lead onboarding, the system shall evaluate customer-linked data stored against Customer ID in DWH, data migration and approved source systems. Annual premium shall be evaluated only after it is captured as a journey input.

### 6.4 Preconditions

Authorized user; valid customer/lead context; active rule configuration; required source data; valid employee and branch mapping; applicable approver mapping where approval is required.

### 6.5 Applicability Conditions

Life Insurance leads that meet one or more active source-defined conditions.

### 6.6 Rule Trigger Conditions

A rule is evaluated only after all data required for that rule is available. Triggered rules are retained and considered before final approval treatment is determined.

### 6.7 Journey Completion Criteria

No applicable breach; required final approval received and journey resumed; direct block; missing-configuration block; or rejection and subsequent configured treatment.

## 7. End-to-End Exception Handling Flow

- The user initiates, saves, resumes or continues the lead onboarding journey.
- Save stores the lead without rule evaluation.
- Start Onboarding evaluates all active rules for which required inputs are currently available.
- Rules requiring later inputs are evaluated only when those inputs become available.
- The High Premium rule is evaluated only after annual premium is captured.
- The system retains all applicable triggered rules for the Lead ID and Customer ID.
- For an approval-enabled breach, the system identifies the employee associated with the transaction.
- The system retrieves the employee branch code and matches it to the hierarchy workbook.
- The system selects Branch Banking, Banca, Persistency, or another configured hierarchy according to the triggered rule.
- The system identifies the configured approver or ordered multi-level sequence. If multiple rules are breached, all breached rules shall be displayed and the approval hierarchy configured at the highest level among those rules shall be followed.
- The raiser enters mandatory remarks, may optionally upload a supporting file, and submits the exception request.
- The current approver receives the request through the Exception Approval Dashboard and configured communications.
- The lead remains pending and cannot progress until all required approval actions are completed.
- Each approver records Approve or Reject with mandatory remarks and may optionally upload a supporting file.
- A non-final approval routes the request to the next configured level.
- Final approval releases the lead and allows the user to resume the customer journey.
- Rejection prevents the blocked journey from proceeding and triggers the configured rejected-lead treatment.
- No applicable rule configuration, hierarchy, employee, branch code, branch match, or required approver results in a non-bypassable block.
- The system audits rule results, hierarchy, approver actions, remarks, dates, times, statuses, notifications and block reasons.
- The exception journey completes only after the final decision and system treatment are completed.
## 8. Detailed Exception Handling Flow

### 8.1 Exception Identification

Identify each breach when all of that rule’s required inputs become available.

### 8.2 Rule Evaluation

Evaluate active configuration and retain all triggered rules. Do not evaluate the premium rule before annual premium is available.

### 8.3 Employee and Branch-Code Identification

Identify the transaction employee and retrieve the employee branch code.

### 8.4 Hierarchy Identification

Match branch code in the hierarchy file and select the hierarchy configured for the rule.

### 8.5 Approval Initiation

Require raiser remarks, allow optional supporting-file upload, prevent duplicate pending request, generate request and assign the first configured approval level.

### 8.6 Multi-Level Approval Processing

Route sequentially through configured approvers. Approvers shall provide mandatory remarks and may optionally upload supporting files. Do not infer a missing or fallback approver.

### 8.7 Approved Outcome

After final approval, update status, notify raiser and enable Resume Journey.

### 8.8 Rejected Outcome

Update status, notify the raiser and block the rejected journey from further progression. A new journey may be created for the rejected lead/customer. Delete the rejected lead after five days as configured.

### 8.9 Pending Approval Outcome

Keep lead locked and visible in the pending bucket; route next level after non-final approval.

### 8.10 Blocked Outcome

Display applicable error, store block reason and prevent bypass.

### 8.11 Resume and Reprocessing

Resume from the latest valid point after final approval. Retry and revalidation treatment shall follow approved open-point decisions.

## 23. Key Business Rules Summary

- Rules execute only when their required data is available.
- High Premium executes only after annual premium is captured.
- All applicable triggered rules are retained for the lead.
- Approvers are derived from employee branch code and the uploaded hierarchy only.
- An inactive or unconfigured rule does not affect the journey. Missing hierarchy, employee, branch or required approver for an active approval-enabled rule blocks the lead without bypass and displays the applicable blocked message.
- Remarks are mandatory for raiser submission and every approval/rejection decision.
- For multiple breached rules, the rule with the highest configured approval hierarchy level is followed.
- Rules, thresholds, hierarchies and approval sequences are configurable by authorized users.
- Final approval is required before journey resume.
- Rejection blocks progression and invokes the configured rejected-lead treatment.
