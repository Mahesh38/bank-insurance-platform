## 2. Purpose

This document defines the business and functional requirements for the Life Insurance Lead Management Module of the new Digital Insurance Platform. The module will allow Bank SPs, Bank Non-SP users and Insurance Relationship Managers (Insurance RMs / FLS) to identify an existing bank customer, capture the customer need, create and assign a lead, optionally schedule a meeting, continue the buying journey, and track the lead until policy outcome.

## 3. Business Objective

- Provide a single lead record for each Life Insurance sales opportunity.
- Enable collaborative lead handling between the Bank SP and Insurance RM.
- Prevent the same logged-in user from creating multiple unfinished leads for the same customer and product type.
- Allow leads to be saved for future action or immediately progressed to suitability assessment.
- Create clear reporting eligibility only after a Benefit Illustration is generated.
- Maintain complete activity and audit history for banking compliance.
## 4. Scope

### 4.1 In Scope

- Manual lead creation for existing bank customers
- Customer search through Customer ID, registered mobile number and PAN
- Customer confirmation with masked mobile number and email ID
- Life Insurance need selection: Term Plan, Savings Plan and ULIP
- Role-specific assignment for Insurance RM, Bank SP and Bank Non-SP
- Platform-generated Lead ID
- Lead deduplication based on logged-in user, customer and product type
- Save & Close and immediate continuation to suitability assessment
- Optional online or in-person meeting scheduling
- SMS and email meeting communication
- Lead reassignment before BI generation
- Lead closure and remarks
- Dashboard-level visibility and action requirements
- Lead stage, status, reporting and audit requirements
- Customer 360, Quote, Insurance Status and Notification integrations
### 4.2 Out of Scope

- Creation of non-bank customers or prospects
- Detailed Lead Dashboard UX and widget design, which may be covered separately
- RM-to-branch and department mapping administration
- Meeting completion or meeting-outcome workflow
- Separate follow-up task management
- Bulk lead upload and campaign lead processing in this version of the screen-wise BRD

## 7. Lead creation Overview

- User opens Lead Creation from the platform.
- User searches for an existing bank customer using Customer ID, mobile number or PAN.
- The Customer 360 API retrieves customer details from CBS.
- User selects and confirms the customer.
- User selects one Life Insurance product type: Term Plan, Savings Plan or ULIP.
- System performs the user-level lead deduplication check.
- Role-specific ownership inputs are captured and the lead is assigned.
- Platform generates the Lead ID and displays the Lead Created screen.
- User may optionally schedule a meeting.
- User either proceeds to Suitability Assessment or selects Save & Close.
- If saved, the lead remains New and appears on the current owners dashboards.
- When the Quote API returns the first BI, the lead becomes Eligible and its stage changes to Quote Generated.
- The same Lead ID continues through proposal and insurer-status stages.
## 8. Role-Specific Lead Creation Flows

### 8.1 Insurance RM Creates the Lead

- The Insurance RM searches and confirms the customer.
- The Insurance RM selects Term, Savings or ULIP.
- The Insurance RM selects the bank branch for which the lead is being created. Only branches available to the logged-in RM shall be shown.
- After branch selection, the platform loads active SPs in the selected branch.
- The Insurance RM selects the SP and continues.
- Lead Generator is the logged-in Insurance RM.
- Lead Fulfiller is the selected Bank SP.
- Both users shall see and action the lead.
### 8.2 Bank SP Creates the Lead

- The SP searches and confirms the customer.
- The SP selects Term, Savings or ULIP.
- The SP does not select a branch because the SP is mapped to one branch. The home branch is derived from the logged-in profile.
- The platform displays active Insurance RMs mapped to the SP branch. Multiple RMs may exist for a branch.
- The SP selects the Insurance RM to whom the lead will be allocated.
- Lead Generator is the logged-in SP.
- Lead Fulfiller is the selected Insurance RM.
- Both users shall see and action the lead.
### 8.3 Bank Non-SP Creates the Lead

- The Non-SP searches and confirms the customer.
- The Non-SP selects Term, Savings or ULIP.
- The Non-SP selects a branch. The branch may be the user home branch or another bank branch.
- The system loads active SPs mapped to the selected branch.
- The Non-SP selects the SP.
- The platform automatically assigns the Insurance RM mapped to the selected branch. The internal department-level RM mapping is outside the scope of this BRD.
- Created By is the logged-in Non-SP.
- Lead Generator is the selected SP.
- Lead Fulfiller is the system-derived Insurance RM.
- After creation and allocation, the lead shall not appear on the Non-SP dashboard.

## 10. Lead Creation and Persistence Rules

### 16.2 Who Can Reassign

The current Bank SP or current Insurance RM who owns and can see the lead may initiate reassignment. The Non-SP cannot reassign because the lead no longer appears on the Non-SP dashboard after allocation.

### 16.3 Reassignment to an SP in the Same Branch

- User selects another active SP in the same branch.
- The branch remains unchanged.
- The Insurance RM remains unchanged.
- Lead Generator and current ownership are updated to the new SP for reporting attribution where applicable.
- The person who relinquishes the applicable ownership loses dashboard visibility; the new owner gains visibility.
- The newly assigned user receives a notification.
### 16.4 Reassignment to an SP in a Different Branch

- User selects another branch and an active SP in that branch.
- The lead branch changes to the newly selected branch.
- The selected SP becomes the current SP / Lead Generator for attribution.
- The Insurance RM mapped to the new branch is assigned automatically.
- Previous owners who are replaced lose dashboard visibility. Current SP and current RM gain visibility.
- The newly assigned user receives a notification.
- All ownership, branch and RM changes are audited.
BR-REASSIGN-001: After BI generation, ownership shall be locked and reassignment controls shall not be available.
