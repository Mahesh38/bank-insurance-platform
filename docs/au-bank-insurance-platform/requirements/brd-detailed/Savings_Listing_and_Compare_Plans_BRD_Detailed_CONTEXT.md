## 2. Purpose

This BRD defines the Savings Plan Compare and Buy journey that begins after Suitability and displays Savings products as dynamic cards. The seller can view all suitable products, access all other Savings products the customer is eligible to buy, change quote inputs, sort and search plans, expand plan options, inspect tax-saving details, compare up to three plans across insurers and select Buy Now to open the Detailed Quote page.

## 3. Business Objectives

- Present all suitable Savings products returned by insurer-wise Suitability mapping.
- Allow the seller to access the wider universe of active Savings products that the customer is eligible to buy.
- Show product benefits in a simple card format that adapts to each product payout structure.
- Allow quote inputs to be changed without editing the completed Suitability Assessment.
- Provide consistent product values across Listing, Compare Plans and Detailed Quote.
- Allow comparison of two or three Savings plans from the same or different insurers.
- Maintain clear audit history of product source, quote version, comparison and Buy Now selection.
## 4. Scope

### 4.1 In Scope

- Customer-specific Savings Plan listing page

## 7. End-to-End Flow

STEP-01: Suitability completes and insurer-wise mapping returns all suitable Savings products.

STEP-02: Platform opens Savings Plans for [Customer Name].

STEP-03: Page displays latest quote-input summary and dynamically calculated Matching Plans count.

STEP-04: Platform requests preliminary quotes for applicable product/plan configurations.

STEP-05: Seller reviews dynamic cards, searches, sorts or changes listing inputs.

STEP-06: Seller may open View All to see the entire eligible Savings product universe.

STEP-07: Seller may expand more plan options under a product.

STEP-08: Seller may compare two or three products or plan options across insurers.

STEP-09: Seller may open View Details without selecting the product for purchase.

STEP-10: Seller selects Buy Now on a specific product/plan option.

STEP-11: Platform saves the current selected product and redirects to Detailed Quote.

#### 8.6.1 Apply Behaviour

- Validate every required input and dependency.
- Refresh product eligibility for Matching Plans and, where relevant, View All.
- Request updated preliminary quotes.
- Rebuild each dynamic card using the latest response.
- Update displayed plan counts and sort order.
- Clear comparison selections whose quote configuration is no longer current.
- Clear a current product selection if it has not been handed to Detailed Quote and its configuration is invalid.
- If one product quote fails, display other successful products and log the failure.
#### 8.6.2 Clear All Behaviour

- Reset Premium Amount, Frequency, PPT, Policy Term and Get Returns As to the latest values confirmed in Suitability.
- Remove post-entry listing filters and restore default configured ordering.
- Do not modify the underlying Suitability Assessment.
- Ask for confirmation only if active comparison or current selection will be cleared.
- Refresh cards and quotes using the restored values.

## 10. Product Ordering and Grouping

- Matching Plans shall show all suitable products; there is no fixed top-five limit.
- The count is dynamic and independent of pagination or progressive loading.
- Sorting changes sequence only and shall not remove eligible products.
- Section labels such as Best Returns and Similar Plans may be configured.
- Similar Plans may reflect different PPT, Policy Term or payout configurations, but the card must clearly show the differing configuration.
- No grouping shall bypass eligibility or make an outside-Suitability product appear as a Suitable Product.
- Default ordering logic shall be configurable.

### 12.4 Different Product Structures

- Lump Sum and Income plans may be compared.
- Use Not Applicable or a dash where a comparison item genuinely does not apply. Do not use zero unless zero is an actual returned value.
- Do not combine periodic income and maturity values unless API returns an approved combined value.
- If a value is unavailable rather than not applicable, show Not Available.
- Every displayed value must retain its quote or product-data source.
## 13. Product Actions

### 13.1 View Details

- View Details opens the Product Details page for the exact product and plan option.
- It does not select the product for purchase.
- It carries Lead ID, Suitability Assessment ID, listing source and current quote reference.
- On return, restore listing inputs, sort, filters, comparison selections and scroll position where technically feasible.
### 13.2 Buy Now

- Buy Now directly opens the Detailed Quote page.
- It saves the exact insurer, product, plan option, listing source and current quote inputs.
- It records whether the product was inside or outside the latest Suitability result.
- It does not generate the final BI by itself.
- Only one product may be the current selected product. A later Buy Now before final BI replaces the earlier selection and preserves the old selection in audit history.
- Compared plans are not automatically selected for purchase.
- Prevent repeated click from creating duplicate selection or quote navigation requests.
- If Detailed Quote cannot open, remain on Listing and do not treat Buy Now as successfully completed.

## 22. Key Business Rules Summary

KBR-01: Matching Plans shows all suitable products; screen counts are illustrative.

KBR-02: View All shows all customer-eligible Savings products, inside or outside Suitability.

KBR-03: Products outside Suitability can be compared and purchased in the current scope.

KBR-04: All listing inputs and quote outputs are dynamic.

KBR-05: Only Annual and Single Premium are offered.

KBR-06: PPT and Policy Term are dynamic.

KBR-07: Get Returns As contains Lump Sum and Income only.

KBR-08: Savings cards change according to product benefit structure.

KBR-09: Annualised Return, multiplier, label and Tax Saving come from API.

KBR-10: No platform-side tax, annualised-return or multiplier calculation is defined.

KBR-11: A product may have multiple expandable plan options.

KBR-12: Two or three plans can be compared, including across insurers.

KBR-13: Comparison is Savings-only.

KBR-14: Compare does not select a product for purchase.

KBR-15: Buy Now directly opens Detailed Quote and does not generate final BI.

KBR-16: Clear All resets to latest Suitability inputs.

KBR-17: Listing input changes do not change Suitability answers.

KBR-18: Stale comparisons and selections are cleared after input or Suitability changes.

KBR-19: View Details, Buy Now and comparisons retain exact product, plan and quote references.

KBR-20: All key actions and failures are audited.
