## 2. Purpose

The Suitability Module enables a Bank SP or Insurance RM to conduct a structured need analysis for the Proposer before recommending Savings Plan and ULIP products. The seller asks a predefined sequence of questions covering the Proposer life stage, risk preference, financial goal, financial profile, indicative premium and preferred tenure. The completed responses are evaluated against an insurer-wise product mapping grid, after which the seller is taken directly to the Suitable Products page.

## 3. Business Objectives

- Capture the Proposer stated needs and financial preferences in a consistent manner.
- Use the same suitability process across Bank SP and Insurance RM users.
- Use insurer-wise rules to identify active Savings and ULIP products suitable for the captured responses.
- Allow the current Bank SP and Insurance RM to jointly work on and resume the same assessment.
- Preserve data entered during Back navigation, Save and Exit, reassignment and handover between current owners.
- Prevent a selected product or final BI from becoming inconsistent with modified suitability responses.
- Maintain complete backend auditability for answers, changes, mapping runs and status transitions.
## 4. Scope

### 4.1 In Scope

- Suitability assessment for Savings Plan and ULIP leads
- Policy-for / Life Assured relationship selection
- Conditional Life Assured details when the policy is not for Self
- Proposer life stage, risk preference and primary goal
- Proposer occupation, education, exact annual income, tobacco response, medical-condition response and existing Life Insurance details
- Prefill and seller editing of available occupation, education, income and existing coverage information
- Tentative premium derivation based initially on Proposer age, annual income and occupation
- Annual Premium and Single Premium frequency selection
- Dynamic Premium Paying Term and Policy Term options
- Save and Exit, Back navigation, resume and shared SP/RM continuation
- Insurer-wise suitability mapping and direct handoff to Suitable Products
- No Suitable Product Found handling
- Editing of completed suitability before final BI generation
- Invalidation of earlier mapping and selected product after a saved suitability change
- Locking suitability after successful final BI generation
- Dashboard status, integration, audit and acceptance requirements
### 4.2 Out of Scope

- Term Plan question set and Term product assessment
- Deviation identification and approval flow shown in the first three screens
- Product-type mismatch screen shown at the end of the screen set
- Seller override to choose a product outside the suitable-product result
- Explanation of why an individual product was recommended or excluded
- Detailed medical, tobacco or lifestyle declarations, which will be captured in the Proposal Form
- Final product comparison, simple quote and Benefit Illustration screen requirements
- Final logic for tentative premium derivation
- Final logic for dynamic Premium Paying Term and Policy Term values
- Final occupation and education master values

## 10. Navigation, Saving and Resume

### 10.1 Back Navigation

- Back navigation shall retain and prefill all previously entered answers.
- Navigating back shall not clear subsequent answers.
- If an earlier saved change makes a dependent response invalid, clear only the affected response and retain all unaffected data.
- The seller shall be informed when a dependent value must be selected again.
- The platform shall not create a duplicate assessment when moving backward and forward.
### 10.2 Save and Exit

- Save and Exit shall be available throughout the question journey.
- Selecting Save and Exit shall automatically save all entered responses.
- No Discard Changes action is required.
- The platform shall store the last visited or next incomplete screen.
- The seller shall return to the Lead Dashboard after successful save.
- The assessment status shall be In Progress.
- No product mapping shall run until all mandatory questions are completed.
### 10.3 Resume

- Either current Bank SP or current Insurance RM may resume the same assessment.
- The assessment shall resume from the last incomplete or last visited screen.
- All saved answers shall be prefilled.
- The same Lead ID and Suitability Assessment ID shall be used.
- If master or dynamic options have changed, retain valid values and clear only invalid selections.

## 13. No Suitable Product Found

- Display a clear No Suitable Product Found message after mapping returns zero products.
- Provide Review Suitability and Save and Exit actions.
- Review Suitability reopens the same assessment with all answers prefilled.
- The platform shall not automatically change answers to produce a match.
- The seller may revise answers only after reconfirming them with the Proposer.
- The seller cannot bypass the mapping or manually select an unsuitable product.
- Save and Exit stores the result and returns to the Lead Dashboard.
- The lead remains pre-BI and is not an Eligible Lead.
## 14. Editing a Completed Assessment

### 14.1 Review Without Change

- Edit Suitability may open a completed assessment until final BI.
- If the user only reviews or navigates, status remains Completed.
- The current mapping and selected product remain valid.
- Unsaved changes do not affect the saved assessment.
### 14.2 Saved Change

- After the first modified answer is successfully saved, status changes to In Progress.
- The previous product mapping is marked Superseded / Invalid.
- Any previously selected product is cleared.
- Any preliminary or simple quote linked to that selection is cleared or marked invalid.
- The platform shall retain unaffected answers.
- Invalid dependent responses shall be cleared and selected again.
- The seller shall complete Suitability again and a new insurer-wise mapping run shall execute.
- Even if the same product is returned again, it shall not be automatically reselected.
### 14.3 Save Failure

- If a modified answer cannot be saved, the assessment remains Completed.
- The previous responses, mapping and selected product remain valid.
- No downstream data is cleared.
- The seller remains on the screen and may retry.
## 15. Lock After Final BI

- Suitability shall lock only after a successful final BI is received from the Quote API.
- Selecting a product, viewing a simple quote or initiating an unsuccessful BI request shall not lock Suitability.
- After lock, Edit Suitability shall not be available.
- Both current Bank SP and Insurance RM may view the assessment but all fields are read-only.
- The selected product and mapping version linked to the final BI remain fixed.
- Lead reassignment also remains blocked as defined in the Lead BRD.
- If final BI generation fails, Suitability remains editable.

## 23. Key Business Rules Summary

KBR-01: Suitability applies only to Savings Plan and ULIP.

KBR-02: Suitability is always performed for the Proposer.

KBR-03: Life Assured may be a non-bank customer, but does not drive this suitability assessment.

KBR-04: The exact screen life-stage, risk and goal options are used.

KBR-05: The seller manually selects life stage, risk preference, primary goal and premium frequency.

KBR-06: The complete Proposer Profile screen moves before Investment Details.

KBR-07: Occupation, Education, Income and Existing Coverage may be prefilled but are editable.

KBR-08: Annual Income and coverage are exact INR amounts.

KBR-09: Tobacco and medical condition capture Yes/No only.

KBR-10: Proposer age, income and occupation initially drive tentative premium suggestion.

KBR-11: Tentative premium is editable.

KBR-12: Only Annual and Single Premium are offered.

KBR-13: PPT and Policy Term are dynamic; Single Premium means Single Pay.

KBR-14: Back retains answers and Save and Exit automatically saves.

KBR-15: Current Bank SP and Insurance RM can jointly continue the same assessment.

KBR-16: Completion directly opens Suitable Products through insurer-wise mapping.

KBR-17: No explanation of product recommendation is required.

KBR-18: No suitable product allows review; no override is permitted.

KBR-19: A completed assessment remains valid indefinitely.

KBR-20: Opening a completed assessment does not change status.

KBR-21: A saved answer change makes status In Progress, invalidates mapping and clears selected product.

KBR-22: Suitability editing is blocked after successful final BI.

KBR-23: Deviation approval and product mismatch are excluded.

KBR-24: All key actions and versions are audited.
